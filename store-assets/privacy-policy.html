Privacy Policy for Case Diary

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Case Diary ("the app") is an offline legal diary app built by Udeshya Kumar.

Information we handle

The app stores the cases, diary entries, dates, notes and settings that you enter. All of this data is stored only on your device in a local database.

No collection or sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. The app has no account system, no backend server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Backup and export

If you use Export or Backup, a file is created on your device and you choose where to share or save it. You can also import a backup file you select. These files are handled only by you and the apps you choose to share them with.

Data deletion

You can delete individual cases and entries, or use Settings → Clear All Data. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
